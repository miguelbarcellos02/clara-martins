About

I am Clara Martins, an architect and urban planner graduated from the Pontifical Catholic University of Minas Gerais (PUC Minas), in Brazil. I hold a postgraduate degree in Interior Design from UNA, in Belo Horizonte (MG), and I am currently pursuing a Master's degree in Public Space Art and Design at the Faculty of Fine Arts of the University of Porto, where I currently live.

In this space, I share artistic processes, reflections and approaches to art and design in both public and private spaces. It brings together a collection of works, experiments and projects developed through different interests and perspectives, exploring the relationships between space, art, design and everyday life.

Pavilhão Pontaletes

2024 · Belo Horizonte – MG, Brazil
Pontifical Catholic University of Minas Gerais

Pavilion designed during our undergraduate studies, in collaboration with Julia Biancini, for the Timber Structures Design course. Developed using a structure composed of wooden poles, metal fittings, and fabric, the project was selected through a competition featuring six proposals created by 8th-semester students. Following a vote, our proposal was chosen, and we had the opportunity to lead a workshop with the students to build the pavilion, which remained installed for four months.

The construction process began with sanding the wooden poles to achieve a uniform finish. They were then cut to the dimensions specified in the template. Each piece was numbered to facilitate assembly, and we created an instruction manual to guide the construction process. After cutting, the pieces were screwed together and coated with three layers of varnish to ensure greater protection, as the pavilion would be installed in an outdoor space. Finally, the fabric was attached to the structure to create shaded areas.
